# Provider logos

White on transparent, for the menubar and dashboard.

- `codex.svg`, `claude.svg`: the `OpenAI` and `ClaudeAI` icons in T3 Code's
  `apps/web/src/components/Icons.tsx` (github.com/pingdotgg/t3code, MIT), recolored to white.
- `opencode.svg`: the OpenCode icon in T3 Code's provider definition, recolored to white; the
  inner block is white at 35% opacity, where the original uses a grey.

The marks themselves belong to OpenAI, Anthropic and OpenCode.
