Gauge concepts: pooled subscriptions

Simulated data. Claude: 3 subscriptions behind cliproxy. Time is 13:20 now. Change the routing mode and burn rate to see how each concept behaves. "%/h" means percent of one account's 5-hour quota per hour.

A. Pool bar (compact: menubar, statusline, tile)

One bar = the whole pool's 5-hour quota. Each colored block is one account's remaining share. Ordered so the block that empties next sits on the right and the bar shrinks from there, exposing the next one.

B. Ribbons (your thickness idea)

One lane per account over the next hours. Thickness = remaining quota. A ribbon thins to nothing at its limit and snaps back to full width at its reset.

C. Runway (detail view: stacked bands plus forecast)

Stacked bands, one per account, total height = pool remaining. Left of "now" is history; right is the forecast at the current burn rate, including the step up at each reset. A red strip marks predicted lockout.

The real app would measure the burn rate from the last 30-60 minutes of polled usage, not a slider.
